# Attached Upgrade Research Notes

## Official reference-link boundary

The converter will present reviewed government **reference links** beside a selected local profile. These links help a Student find the relevant land-record or revenue authority; they do **not** certify a universal local-unit value or replace district/tehsil confirmation.

The Bihar reference was verified at the Government of Bihar Department of Revenue and Land Reforms site. The page identifies the department, its Minister, and its Secretary. [1] The Madhya Pradesh reference was verified at the Government of India Department of Land Resources page, which is owned by the Department of Land Resources, Ministry of Rural Development, and provides a Madhya Pradesh document. [2]
